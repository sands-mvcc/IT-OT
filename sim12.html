Voice Coil Actuator

A single-phase, direct-drive electromagnetic linear actuator using a permanent-magnet field structure and a lightweight coil winding.

OSCILLATING MOTION

Cycle Controls

Typical Purposes

High-frequency sorting, micro-stamping, optical focusing, force-feedback controls, wire bonding.

How It Operates

  1. Current (I) flows through a coil of length (L) placed inside a magnetic field (B).
  2. The Lorentz force F = B·I·L generates a proportional mechanical force on the coil.
  3. Reversing current direction instantly reverses the direction of movement, with no mechanical hysteresis.
