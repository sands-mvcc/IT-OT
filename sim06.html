Geneva Drive (Maltese Cross)

A gear mechanism that converts continuous rotary motion into precise, intermittent rotary motion.

Live simulation — not to scale
INTERMITTENT MOTION

Cycle Controls

Typical Purposes

Automated indexing tables, rotary assembly stations, packaging machinery, intermittent conveyor feeds.

How It Operates

  1. A continuously rotating drive wheel carries an offset pin and a locking disc.
  2. The pin slides into a radial slot on the driven cross, advancing it a fraction of a turn (90 deg for a 4-slot wheel).
  3. While the pin is outside a slot, the locking disc holds the driven wheel perfectly stationary.
