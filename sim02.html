ACT-02 · LINEAR MOTION

Hydraulic Cylinder

A heavy-duty fluid power actuator that uses pressurized, incompressible hydraulic oil to generate very high linear forces.

Live simulation — not to scale
LINEAR MOTION

Cycle Controls

Typical Purposes

Stamping presses, forging, injection-molding clamps, heavy lifting, workpiece manipulation.

How It Operates

  1. A hydraulic pump forces oil into the cylinder barrel at 1,000-5,000+ PSI.
  2. Pressurized oil acts directly on the piston face, producing linear thrust.
  3. Control valves modulate fluid direction; returning oil is routed back to the reservoir.

Specifications

  • Power sourceHigh-pressure oil
  • Force outputVery high
  • SpeedLow to medium
  • PrecisionLow to medium
