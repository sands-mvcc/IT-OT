Threat Actor Landscape

🌏 Nation-State Actors
▼

Primary Actors: Russia, China, Iran, North Korea

  • Russia: Targeting critical infrastructure, energy sectors
  • China: Industrial espionage, supply chain compromises
  • Iran: Disruptive attacks on nuclear and energy facilities
  • North Korea: Financial motivation, cryptocurrency theft

Capabilities: Advanced persistent threats (APTs), zero-day exploits, long-term persistence

💰 Cybercriminal Groups
▼

Focus: Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) operators

  • BlackBasta, LockBit, RansomHouse affiliates
  • Manufacturing sector most targeted (14% of ransomware events)
  • Average ransom payments: $1.5 million in 2024
  • 87% increase in OT-targeting ransomware attacks

Impact: Operational shutdowns, data theft, extortion

🏴‍☠️ Hacktivists
▼

Motivation: Political messaging, disruption of critical infrastructure

  • 15% of OT attacks with physical consequences
  • Targeting water treatment facilities
  • Energy grid disruptions
  • Geopolitically motivated campaigns

Methods: DDoS attacks, defacement, system disruption

👨‍💼 Insider Threats
▼

Types: Privileged access abuse, negligent actions

  • Accidental misconfigurations
  • Malicious insiders with access to critical systems
  • Social engineering targeting OT personnel
  • USB-based malware introduction

Challenge: Difficult to detect, high trust levels

Common Attack Vectors

48%
IT network breaches leading to OT compromise
75%
Industrial controllers with unpatched vulnerabilities
🌐 IT Network Breaches
▼

Primary pathway (48% of OT incidents):

  • Lateral movement from compromised IT systems
  • Inadequate network segmentation
  • Shared credentials between IT and OT
  • Converged networks without proper isolation

Prevention: Network segmentation, zero-trust architecture

📧 Phishing Attacks
▼

Targeting OT personnel:

  • Spear-phishing with industrial themes
  • Malicious attachments (Excel, PDF with macros)
  • Credential harvesting for OT access
  • Business email compromise (BEC)

Impact: Initial access to secure OT networks

🔗 Supply Chain Compromises
▼

Affecting industrial equipment:

  • Compromised vendor software updates
  • Malicious firmware in industrial devices
  • Third-party remote access tools
  • Cloud service provider breaches

Challenge: Trusted relationships exploited

🔓 Remote Access Vulnerabilities
▼

In converged environments:

  • Unsecured VPN connections
  • Default credentials on remote access tools
  • Weak multi-factor authentication
  • Internet-exposed HMI interfaces

Risk: Direct access to control systems

🏗️ Legacy System Exploitation
▼

Due to unpatched vulnerabilities:

  • Decades-old systems without security updates
  • Proprietary protocols with known flaws
  • Default passwords and configurations
  • No encryption capabilities

Challenge: Cannot be easily updated or replaced
