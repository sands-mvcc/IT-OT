ACT-04 · LINEAR MOTION

Solenoid

A compact electromechanical device producing short-stroke linear or angular motion through electromagnetic induction.

Live simulation — not to scale
LINEAR MOTION

Cycle Controls

Typical Purposes

Directional control valves, mechanical latching, high-speed sorting gates, circuit breakers.

How It Operates

  1. A wire coil wound around a hollow tube surrounds a movable ferromagnetic plunger.
  2. Energizing the coil creates a magnetic field that pulls the plunger toward the coil's center.
  3. De-energizing the coil lets an internal return spring push the plunger back to its default position.

Specifications

  • Power sourceElectricity
  • Force outputLow
  • SpeedHigh (short stroke)
  • PrecisionBinary (on/off)
