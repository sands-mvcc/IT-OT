Industrial IoT & Actuators Overview

Interactive simulations of industrial automation devices that convert digital signals into physical actions

What are Actuators in Industrial IoT?

Actuators are devices that convert digital control signals into physical actions in automated environments. They work alongside sensors and controllers to create responsive industrial systems.
