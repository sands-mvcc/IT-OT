Workshop Overview

As digital transformation accelerates across industries, the integration of traditional IT systems with operational technology creates both opportunities and significant security challenges. This workshop equips faculty with the knowledge, practical skills, and teaching materials needed to educate the next generation of cybersecurity professionals.

🎯

Learning Outcomes

Define IT/OT systems, analyze threats, apply security frameworks, and design practical exercises using Cisco Packet Tracer for real-world scenarios.

🔧

Hands-on Training

Four comprehensive Cisco Packet Tracer exercises covering manufacturing, water treatment, logistics, and incident response scenarios.

📚

Industry Cases

Real-world case studies from manufacturing, wastewater treatment, and logistics sectors with detailed analysis and teaching materials.

Hands-on Exercises

Exercise 1: Smart Manufacturing Floor ▼

Duration: 2 hours

Components: PLCs, industrial switches, SCADA workstations, IoT sensors

Learning Focus: Network segmentation, industrial protocols, monitoring systems

Assessment: Proper IT/OT segmentation, security controls implementation

Exercise 2: Water Treatment SCADA System ▼

Duration: 2 hours

Components: Flow sensors, chemical dosing pumps, alarm systems, HMI interfaces

Learning Focus: Safety-critical systems, emergency procedures, public health protection

Assessment: Safety system design, alarm configuration, incident response

Exercise 3: Smart Logistics Hub ▼

Duration: 2 hours

Components: Warehouse automation, GPS tracking, environmental sensors, ERP integration

Learning Focus: Supply chain visibility, cold chain management, third-party integration

Assessment: End-to-end integration, security controls, scalability design

Exercise 4: Cybersecurity Incident Response ▼

Duration: 2 hours

Scenarios: Ransomware lateral movement, industrial espionage, critical infrastructure attacks

Learning Focus: Threat detection, forensic analysis, coordinated response, recovery procedures

Assessment: Response speed, containment effectiveness, team coordination
