ACT-05 · ROTARY MOTION

Stepper Motor

A brushless DC motor that divides one full rotation into many equal steps (typically 200 steps / 1.8 degrees per step).

Live simulation — not to scale
ROTARY MOTION

Cycle Controls

Typical Purposes

3D printing, conveyor indexing, component feeders, low-to-medium speed pick-and-place, peristaltic pumps.

How It Operates

  1. Electromagnetic coils around a central rotor are energized in a controlled sequence.
  2. The rotor's magnets align with each energized stator coil in turn.
  3. The rotor advances in discrete, repeatable angular steps without needing closed-loop feedback.

Specifications

  • Power sourceElectricity
  • Force outputLow to medium
  • SpeedLow to medium
  • PrecisionMedium to high
