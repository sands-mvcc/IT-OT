Pneumatic Cylinder

A fluid power device that converts energy from compressed air into linear mechanical force and motion using a piston within a cylindrical housing.

Live simulation — not to scale
LINEAR MOTION

Cycle Controls

Typical Purposes

Pick-and-place, clamping, pushing, ejecting, pressing, high-speed gate operation.

How It Operates

  1. Compressed air enters through an inlet port and presses against the piston face.
  2. Pressure drives the piston rod outward on the extend stroke.
  3. Double-acting cylinders route air to the opposite chamber to retract the rod; single-acting types rely on a spring or the load.

Specifications

  • Power sourceCompressed air
  • Force outputMedium
  • SpeedHigh
  • PrecisionLow
