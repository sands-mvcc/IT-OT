🖥️ About These Simulators

Read first

Every simulator below runs entirely in the browser — nothing to install and no live equipment involved. They're designed as drop-in demonstrations or student self-check activities for a solid-state devices or digital electronics unit. Click any card to launch the corresponding simulator page.
