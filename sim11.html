How Strain Wave Gearing Works

  1. Wave Generator (Input): Elliptical cam surrounded by a flexible ball bearing rotates, continuously flexing the thin flexspline.
  2. Tooth Engagement: Deforms external flexspline teeth to engage fixed circular spline teeth along the major elliptical axis ($180^\circ$ apart).
  3. Tooth Disengagement: At the minor elliptical axis, teeth completely disengage, permitting smooth differential motion.
  4. Differential Tooth Count: Flexspline has 2 fewer teeth than Circular Spline ($\Delta N = -2$). Each WG turn rotates Flexspline backward by 2 teeth.
